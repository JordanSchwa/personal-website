"[CSM](https://csmentors.studentorg.berkeley.edu/#/) is a student-run organization that provides guidance and resources in a smaller classroom environment through group tutoring sessions."

CS C88C, also known as Data C88C, covers similar topics to CS61A, but from a data science perspective. It is generally considered a CS or Data 1.5 course.
