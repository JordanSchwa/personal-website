Jordan Schwartz is a current second-year Computer and Information Science masters student at the University of Pennsylvania. She graduated from UC Berkeley in the fall of 2024 with a major in Cognitive Science and a minor in Computer Science. 

Jordan is actively looking for a full time role in higher education with a teaching position starting in summer or fall 2027. 

Jordan's history in teaching higher ed CS and DS extends from intro and Python to computer security to ML theory/linear algebra and probability. Her experience in teaching introductory computer science really drove her passion for equitable education in CS. More specifically, Jordan has experience working with transfer students and non-traditional students in order to lower extraordinary barriers to success.

Jordan is also currently working towards a master's thesis in HCI with her advisor, Talie Massachi.
